**Pattern notes (new this pass):** (1) **the "announced by" trap now
hits three MP premiums in one bucket** — a season's premium is pinned
by the page that carries its "Monthly Mission Pass Update" section,
but that section always says "refreshed on the first day of <month>",
and the section can sit on the *preceding* event's page (all three
cases here). (2) **collab events can carry a wrong pre-announcement
date** — the Persona on Frontline block was recorded at 08-12 (an
early list value) while every primary source says 08-13; the
MP-premium statements and the infobox were the tie-breakers. (3)
**the MP premium pattern now has 32 confirmed instances** (S46, S47,
S48 added this pass; the wiki's explicit "first day of" statements
for September and October are the second and third confirmations of
the monthly cadence). (4) **chibi packs follow the event, not the
featured characters** — the Smol Yukiko model ships 08-13, a week
before Yukiko's own 08-20 banner wave (same shape as the B14 Smol
Mint/Prika cases). (5) **collab costume gating reuses the
character-gate rule** — the S.E.E.S. challenge runs from 08-13, but
Yukiko's Amagi Inn Kimono is first obtainable on her 08-20 banner
open, exactly like B13's Takina DA Training Wear (Second).
