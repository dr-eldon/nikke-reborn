# NIKKE REBORN
A completely new and built-from-scratch NIKKE content pack for the people of culture.
Inspired by [the amazing old modpack by daidangodan](https://www.nexusmods.com/tcgcardshopsimulator/mods/667).

> **Disclaimer**
> Heavily in development. Expect breaking changes!
> I have just finished setting up most of the infrastructure to generate content for the mod, so expect updates often! If I can stop playing.
>
> Coming up next: the TETRA expansion!

## New Content (for now)
- ELYSION — an expansion featuring 19 NIKKEs from the original launch + 2 skins, with 16 different rarities. Totaling 244 cards; many animated!

![ELYSION Expansion](images/expansions/ELYSION.png)

## Roadmap
- +10 different expansions — featuring different NIKKEs, new skins, and events; ordered by release date.
- Accessories — sleeves, deck boxes, playmats, comics, and more!
- Figurines — the chibi plushies must come back.
- Posters — plenty of content to generate many of them.
- Shop Theme — a texture pack to completely change your shop into a NIKKE shop.

## Requirements
- BepInEx
- [Enhanced Prefab Loader](https://www.nexusmods.com/tcgcardshopsimulator/mods/2)
- [EPL Card Animator](https://www.nexusmods.com/tcgcardshopsimulator/mods/1100)
